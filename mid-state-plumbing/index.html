Careers

The current website posts one full-time opening: a commercial construction plumber who leads a two-person crew on commercial job sites.

Commercial Construction Plumber

Full-time

Pay: $25.00 – $30.00 per hour

The posted role seeks an experienced and reliable lead plumber to head a two-person team on commercial construction job sites, with a background in commercial plumbing, leadership, and a commitment to safety and quality workmanship.

The role covers daily operations on site, coordination with general contractors and subcontractors, and completing plumbing tasks according to plan.

Key responsibilities

  • Lead a two-person plumbing crew on commercial job sites
  • Interpret and work from blueprints, drawings, and specifications
  • Install, modify, and repair plumbing systems including water supply, waste and vent systems
  • Ensure all work meets local plumbing codes, safety standards, and project specifications
  • Coordinate workflow, material needs, and manpower to meet project deadlines
  • Communicate with project managers, other trades, and site supervisors
  • Maintain accurate records of work completed, materials used, and time spent on tasks
  • Troubleshoot issues and provide solutions during construction and alterations
  • Train and mentor crew members, with quality control and adherence to procedures
  • Ensure proper use and maintenance of tools and equipment
  • Enforce and follow all safety policies and procedures

Requirements

  • 3 years of commercial plumbing experience
  • Strong knowledge of plumbing codes, standards, and best practices
  • Ability to read and interpret construction documents and blueprints
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication skills
  • Reliable transportation to and from DeBary, FL, and a valid driver’s license
  • Ability to lift 50+ lbs, climb ladders, and work in the conditions the job site requires
  • Previous leadership or supervisory experience (preferred)
  • OSHA 10 or 30 certification (preferred)
  • As a condition of employment, selected candidates must successfully pass a pre-employment drug screening in accordance with the drug-free workplace policy.

Language

  • English (required)
  • Spanish (preferred)

Benefits

  • Company truck
  • Retirement plan
  • Health insurance
  • Dental insurance
  • Life insurance
  • Paid time off

How to apply

To be considered for employment, applicants must complete an employment application and attend an in-person interview at the office. Interviews are conducted by appointment only, Monday through Thursday, between 8:30 AM and 3:30 PM. Call the office in advance to schedule. The employment application is available as a fillable PDF, and a printed application can be provided at the office.
