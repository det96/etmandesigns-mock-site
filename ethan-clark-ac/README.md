# Ethan Clark Air Conditioning — concept redesign

Private pitch mock-up for EtmanDesigns (David Etman). This is not the live site, it is not deployed, and it does not use analytics.

Preview it the way David will: double-click `index.html` so it opens in desktop Chrome (`file://`). No build step and no network fonts. Inter is self-hosted in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License (see `fonts/Inter-OFL.txt`).

The page sends `noindex, nofollow`. Do not turn on GitHub Pages for this folder.

Email on this concept stays `ethanclarkac@gmail.com`.

Still a placeholder or a sample:

- Shop exterior at 1320 Rothwell Street, service trucks, and technician photos. Not published on ethanclark.com. The frame says so. No stock photo is used.
- The person in the text-message mockup is an illustrative silhouette, labeled Illustrative. It is not a photo of their technician.
- Hours. Still “Hours to be confirmed.” A public place page listed hours, including Thursday “8 AM–5 AM,” which does not match the other weekdays. Those hours are not shown.
- Map. Still “Map to be added.”
- Optional add-on block (maintenance plan, financing, seasonal tune-up). Badged “Optional add-on (sample).” “Ask about…” only. The site lists maintenance and tune-ups as services. It does not publish a plan, financing terms, or a seasonal offer.
- Google review count stays 5.0 from 173, matching this brief and the place page above. Another directory said 174. The hero was not changed.
- Request form. Still a non-submitting demo.
- Logo and favicon. Still the stand-in mark.
- River Oaks. Excluded. It is not in the service-area list on https://ethanclark.com/ (Bellaire and West University Place are).

The live privacy policy and terms (https://ethanclark.com/privacy.html and https://ethanclark.com/terms.html) list `eclarkac@gmail.com`. This pitch keeps `ethanclarkac@gmail.com`, which is also the email on the public place page.

Do not claim 24/7 or emergency service. ethanclark.com does not say that.
