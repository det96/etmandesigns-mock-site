Since 1987

Joseph started Ethan Clark Air Conditioning in 1987. This is the shop’s own history, from the current about page.

  1. 1987 Joseph starts Ethan Clark Air Conditioning.
  2. 1990 The second generation starts full time, the summer after graduating from Stratford High School in west Houston, including its HVAC vocational program. Early work includes large commercial jobs and ocean-going freighter ships.
  3. Mid-1990s The shop is built downtown, off I-10.
  4. Inside the loop As housing development inside the loop grows, the company moves into residential service and repair.
  5. 1998 The second generation becomes service manager, still works in the field, and is involved in every installation.
  6. Office Melissa is the office manager.
