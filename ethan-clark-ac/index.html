Family business since 1987

From the current about page, in the shop’s own words.

My father, Joseph, started Ethan Clark Air Conditioning in 1987. My full time career began with Ethan Clark in 1990, the summer I graduated.

I attended Stratford High School in west Houston and was also fortunate to train in the HVAC vocational program during my Junior and Senior year.

Most of my work when I started out was on large commercial jobs and ocean going freighter ships.

After we built our shop downtown off I-10 in the mid 90's, the explosion of housing development inside the loop began. We started to transition into residential service and repair. By 1998 I became the service manager of Ethan Clark. Till this day, I still work in the field and am involved in every installation we do.

My wife Melissa is also the office manager…
